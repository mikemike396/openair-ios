# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

OpenAir: Window Weather (“OpenAir,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) helps users decide when outdoor conditions are suitable for opening windows.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Use

OpenAir may use the following information:

### Location Data

OpenAir uses your location to retrieve local weather conditions and provide window recommendations.

Location may be used to determine:
- Outdoor temperature
- Humidity
- Dew point
- Wind conditions
- Rain or precipitation conditions

Location data is used only to provide app functionality. OpenAir does not sell your location data.

### Weather Data

OpenAir uses weather data to generate recommendations about whether conditions are good for opening your windows.

Weather data may include:
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Dew point
- Wind speed
- Rain or precipitation
- Forecast conditions

### Notifications

If you enable notifications, OpenAir may use local weather conditions and your preferences to send alerts about window conditions.

Notifications are optional and can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

### Diagnostics and Crash Data

OpenAir may receive limited diagnostic or crash information through Apple or other app services to help identify bugs, improve performance, and maintain app reliability.

This information is used only to improve the app.

## How We Use Information

OpenAir uses information to:

- Provide local weather-based window recommendations
- Send optional notifications
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Troubleshoot bugs and crashes
- Maintain app functionality

## Data Sharing

OpenAir does not sell your personal information.

OpenAir may share limited information with service providers only as needed to operate the app, such as weather data providers, crash reporting tools, diagnostics tools, analytics tools, or Apple services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## Third-Party Services

OpenAir may use third-party services to provide weather data, diagnostics, analytics, or app functionality.

Depending on the app version, these may include:

- Apple WeatherKit
- Apple App Store services
- Apple notification and diagnostic services

If additional services are added, this Privacy Policy should be updated.

## Data Storage

OpenAir may store app preferences, selected location information, and notification settings locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

## Children’s Privacy

OpenAir is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Choices

You can control app permissions through your device settings.

You may:

- Disable location access
- Disable notifications
- Delete the app to remove locally stored app data
- Manage app privacy permissions in iOS Settings

Some app features may not work properly without location access.

## Data Retention

OpenAir retains locally stored information for as long as the app remains installed on your device, unless you delete or reset that data.

Diagnostic or crash data may be retained by Apple or other service providers according to their own retention policies.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
