# OpenAir: Window Weather

**Know when to open your windows.**

OpenAir is a simple iOS app that uses local weather conditions to help you decide when fresh air is worth it.

It checks outdoor temperature, humidity, dew point, rain, and wind, then gives you a clear recommendation so you do not have to interpret a full weather report.
